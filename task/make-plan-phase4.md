# PHASE 4 — INTERVIEW CIRCUIT + OFFER (W37–W48 | Feb 1 – Apr 30, 2027)

**Phase goal:** Convert pipeline into offers, negotiate, choose, onboard.
**Success criteria for Final Gate (W48, Apr 30, 2027):**
- ≥ 1 offer accepted at ≥ $5,000/month (target $7,000+)
- Offer is good fit (team, tech, growth)
- Onboarding plan ready
- Phase 1-4 retrospective documented

**Calendar overview:**
```
M10 (Feb 2027)  W37 W38 W39 W40  — Full interview mode, take-homes, finals
M11 (Mar 2027)  W41 W42 W43 W44  — Final rounds, negotiation, decision
M12 (Apr 2027)  W45 W46 W47 W48  — Accept, prepare onboarding, retrospective
```

**Critical mindset shift in Phase 4:**
- Build mode = OFF. Polish mode = ON.
- Quality of interview prep > quantity of new code.
- Energy management > coding hours.
- Multiple offers = leverage, single offer = limited choices.
- Don't ghost any company — even rejected ones can refer you later.
